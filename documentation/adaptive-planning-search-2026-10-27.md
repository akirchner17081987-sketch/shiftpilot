# Adaptive automatic planning

Automatic background jobs now separate constructive allocation from checked
whole-block repair. All companies use their own snapshot, permissions, wishes,
fixed duties, personal ceilings, rest requirements and staffing requirements.

For confirmed policies the first pass allocates scarce mandatory resources
without enumerating the large daily critical beam first. It keeps priority and
fixed rosters. Up to 90 seconds are reserved for initial allocation; remaining
time is available for block repair. Every month receives its own budget, up to
eight minutes, with one additional three-minute extension when at most five
positions remain and checked improvements are still being found. Large requests share a maximum 50-minute budget, below job expiry.
Each invocation saves a serializable checkpoint. Closing the browser does not
cancel the job. The worker performs a second bounded solver pass only when the
first pass leaves CPU headroom.

Repair alternates diverse neighbourhoods with employees eligible for a current
gap and the incumbent owners of resources needed by a replacement block.
Stagnation broadens the neighbourhood, including occasional whole-crew passes.
Only a complete, independently checked candidate can replace the incumbent.
Coverage precedes workload preferences. Preferred block lengths are considered
without converting them into a new hard rule or forbidding month-edge fragments.

A valid incumbent can consist of approved smaller options joined into a block
which is absent from the capped option list. Such a block becomes an incumbent
column if all its duties already appear among that employee's permitted options.
Required indivisible work/rest options are excluded from this reconstruction.
Missing or unauthorized candidates do not gain new permissions through repair.

After an improving but incomplete round, the worker can rebuild the search from
the best checked proposal twice. Rebuilding retains at least the same total coverage and the
original fixed/base duties. Conditional staffing which cannot be represented by
the interval model uses the existing scoped, checked replacement search.

The progress detail reports remaining and closed positions. Search limits remain
visible in result metadata. Complete coverage does not waive personal SOLL
shortfall review, approved wishes, permissions, rest or company constraints.
Unfilled positions after a bounded search are not a proof of personnel shortage
or mathematical infeasibility. No plan is published by calculating a proposal.

Valid incomplete proposals with the same company, period and fingerprint are also
available as repair references. Every duty is rechecked against the new job,
including server-confirmed wishes. A normal retry starts from that checked partial
plan instead of discarding its completed coverage. Explicit full rebuilds retain
their existing meaning. A reference never authorizes saving or publication.

During an incomplete repair a vacancy may move along an augmenting chain. The
total number of vacancies and critical vacancies is bounded by the checked
incumbent. This permits multi-step repairs without requiring every chain to fit
inside one neighbourhood. Complete coverage remains protected by a zero bound.
